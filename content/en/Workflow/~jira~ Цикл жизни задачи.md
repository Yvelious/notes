## Task Lifecycle

The task lifecycle is a sequence of states through which a task passes, starting from the moment of creation and ending with its completion.

There is no universal standard that is accepted by all companies. Each company can define its own task lifecycle that aligns with its processes and development methodology.

Below is an example of the task lifecycle that I use in my work. It is not mandatory and can be modified according to the project's needs.

**The task lifecycle consists of the following states:**
1. **Created** - the task is created, but not assigned to anyone. It sits in the general task list (Backlog).
2. **To Do** - the task is ready to be executed, and an assignee is attached. It is waiting for its turn.
3. **In Progress** - the task is currently being worked on.
4. **Ready for Review** - the task is completed and ready for review.
5. **In Review** - the task is under review.
6. **Ready for Testing** - the task has been reviewed and is ready for testing.
7. **In Testing** - the task is being tested.
8. **Ready for Deploy** - the task has been tested and is ready for deployment.
9. **In Deploy** - the task is in production.
10. **Done** - the task is completed.

This is the sequence of states through which a task passes, starting from the moment of creation and ending with its completion.

There are also states that can be added to a task at any stage of its lifecycle, regardless of its current state:
1. **Blocked** - the task is blocked and cannot be completed for some reason. This usually occurs when the execution of the task depends on another task that has not yet been completed.
2. **On Hold** - the task is paused. For example, if a higher priority task comes up.
3. **Canceled** - the task is canceled. If the task is no longer relevant.
