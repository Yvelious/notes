## Task Life Cycle

The task life cycle is the sequence of states through which a task passes, starting from the moment of creation and ending with its completion.

There is no universal standard that is accepted by all companies. Each company can define its own task life cycle, which will correspond to its processes and development methodology.

Below is an example of a task life cycle that I use in my work. It is not mandatory and can be modified according to the project's needs.

**The task life cycle consists of the following states:**
1. **Created** - the task has been created, but it has not been assigned to anyone yet. It lies in the general task list (Backlog).
2. **To Do** - the task is ready to be executed and has been assigned to someone. It's awaiting its turn.
3. **In Progress** - the task is in the process of being executed.
4. **Ready for Review** - the task is completed and ready for review.
5. **In Review** - the task is under review.
6. **Ready for Testing** - the task has been reviewed and is ready for testing.
7. **In Testing** - the task is being tested.
8. **Ready for Deploy** - the task has been tested and is ready for deployment.
9. **In Deploy** - the task is in production.
10. **Done** - the task is completed.

This is the sequence of states through which a task passes, starting from the moment of creation and ending with its completion.

There are also states that can be added to a task at any stage of its life cycle, regardless of its current status:
1. **Blocked** - the task is blocked and cannot be executed for some reason. Usually when the execution of a task depends on another task that has not yet been completed.
2. **On Hold** - the task is on hold. For example, if a higher-priority task comes up during work.
3. **Canceled** - the task is canceled. If the task is no longer relevant.
